**A type decides who catches your eye. A relationship pattern decides how it feels once you're dating them, and that's the part that repeats.** When people say "I keep dating the same type," they rarely mean the same hair colour. They mean the same story: the same chase, the same distance, the same doubts at three months.

## What actually repeats

Look back at your last few relationships and ignore looks for a moment. What was the first month like? Did you spend it wondering whether they were as into you as you were into them? Did you start to feel crowded once they got serious? Did you keep one foot out the door, even when you liked them?

That first-month feeling is the thing to track. It comes from your relationship pattern, which Bearing measures with two scores: how much you worry about whether a relationship is safe, and how uneasy closeness makes you. The [four patterns and how they're scored](/blog/attachment-style-quiz) are explained in full on their own page. Here is how each one tends to choose partners.

## How each pattern picks

**Attached** people want closeness and go after it, but they worry about whether it's mutual. They often end up with partners who are a little hard to read, because the uncertainty feels like excitement. The repeat looks like this: a strong start, then months of trying to get a clear answer.

**Independent** people are comfortable alone and get uneasy when someone wants more than they do. They often end up with partners who want more closeness than they can give, or they lose interest right as things get serious. The repeat looks like this: a good few months, then a slow step back.

**Cautious** people want closeness and protect themselves from it at the same time. They're drawn in, then pull away once it starts to feel real, and they're often drawn to partners who are on and off too. The repeat looks like this: push, pull, and a lot of mixed signals on both sides.

**Open** people find closeness fairly easy, so their "type" tends to come from tastes and values more than from an old feeling being replayed. If you're Open and still see a repeat, it's worth looking at things outside the pattern, like where you meet people.

## Why the wrong one feels like chemistry

The person who sets off your pattern often gives the strongest spark, because they feel familiar. Someone who replies slowly and then shows up warmly gives an Attached person a feeling they know well. A partner who never asks for much feels like freedom to an Independent person.

The steady person who replies on time and says what they mean can feel a bit flat by comparison at first. That flat feeling is worth a second look before you write them off. Sometimes it just means nothing is setting off your pattern.

## Breaking the loop while dating

- **Name your first-month feeling.** Write down what you felt early on in your last two or three relationships. If the same word keeps coming up, like "unsure," "crowded" or "hooked," that's your pattern.
- **Slow down the start.** Patterns run fastest when things move quickly. A few extra weeks before calling it serious gives you time to see the person, not just the feeling.
- **Notice your move, not theirs.** The moment you start chasing a reply, or the moment you want to leave because it's going well, is the moment to pause.
- **Give the calm ones another date.** If someone feels safe but not thrilling, see them once more before deciding.
